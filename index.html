BEFORE YOU START

Common questions

Do my survey responses leave my browser?

The tools process uploaded CSV contents in your browser and do not send them to an analysis server. You can download reports locally. Hosting providers still serve the website; email enquiries use your email provider.

Can I upload an Excel workbook?

Not directly. Export a UTF-8, comma-separated CSV first. The simple checker accepts up to 10 MiB; the advanced checker has a 50 MiB limit, with files below 10 MiB recommended.

Does a flagged record need to be deleted?

No. Repeated patterns, missing values or range issues require contextual review. The advanced tool can simulate explicit exclusions; it does not modify the original file.

Can it check Likert scales or multiple-choice questions?

The advanced tool supports integer-scale thresholds and multiple responses stored as one binary 0/1 column per option. The simplified checker counts exact single-choice responses. Neither tool interprets multiple options combined in one cell.

Does a matching percentage prove my statement is true?

No. Arithmetic can match while the wording describes a different concept or a broader population. Questionnaire meaning and sampling evidence need separate human review.

Is the whole website available in nine languages?

The simplified Claim Checker has nine interface dictionaries. Advanced Research Check and other pages are currently in English. Translation review is ongoing.
