WORKING PAPER · OBSERVATIONAL DATA

Household Income, Age and Family Composition in Relation to Food-Related Worry

Analysis of Food and You 2 Wave 6 survey data examining associations with food-related worry. The analysis is observational and does not establish causation.

Methods: descriptive statistics and logistic regression. Survey design and weighting limitations must be considered when interpreting estimates.

IN PROGRESS · NOT YET VALIDATED

Sustainable Consumption and Consumer Inertia

Exploration of consumer intentions and switching barriers in sustainable fashion using secondary survey data and PLS-SEM.

Status: data provenance and measurement validity issues are still under review. No disputed empirical findings are presented as established results.

METHODS DEMONSTRATION · SYNTHETIC DATA

Monte Carlo Simulation of Random Assignment

A simulation exercise illustrating sampling variability under an assumed effect. Synthetic observations are used; this is not a real-world experiment or evidence of a consumer effect.
