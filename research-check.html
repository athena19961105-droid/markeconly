6 · Unified claim-to-evidence audit (v4.1.1)

Check whether a proposed statement is consistent with the selected survey response. This is a structured self-audit, not automatic semantic verification.

Important: Measurement type and claim type are declared by the user, not inferred or validated from questionnaire wording. Results do not establish representativeness, causality, or construct validity.

7 · Likert / Top-2 Box evidence (v4.2 beta)

Calculate the share choosing the highest two scale categories. Show exactly which responses count and which denominator is used. This is descriptive, unweighted analysis; it does not establish construct validity or population representativeness.

Only integer responses inside the configured scale count as valid. Blank values are missing; text, decimals, and out-of-range values are invalid. No automatic recoding, reverse coding, weighting, or row exclusions from Section 5 are applied.

Upload a CSV in Section 1 to begin.

8 · Claim–Question Mapping (v4.3 beta)

Connect a proposed public statement to the exact questionnaire wording and a CSV column. This is a structured self-audit: construct types are declared by you, not automatically inferred from text.

Important: A matching label is not proof of construct validity. Use Sections 4 and 7 for numerical evidence; this module does not calculate or verify percentages.

9 · Multiple-response & denominator audit (v4.4)

For check-all-that-apply questions stored as one binary column per option (1 = selected, 0 = not selected). This does not parse comma-separated choices in one cell. Select two or more option columns.

Upload a CSV in Section 1 first.

A response set is complete only when every selected column contains exactly 0 or 1. Partial blanks and invalid codes are excluded from option counts. No automatic interpretation of blanks as 0.
