Illustrative Sample Research Brief

Illustrative example — not an empirical market report. This page demonstrates how a future client brief would be structured. It does not contain verified market statistics, original consumer interviews or actual competitor findings. The hypothetical brand and decision below are fictional.
Example category: sustainable apparelExample market: United KingdomFormat: desk research

Example research question

What publicly documented barriers should a hypothetical independent sustainable clothing brand investigate before setting its pricing and product messaging for UK customers?

1. Decision context

The hypothetical business wants to understand whether price, trust, perceived product quality or convenience may influence purchase decisions. These are candidate research themes, not measured findings for the brand.

2. Evidence collection plan

  1. Define target segment, product category and intended price range.
  2. Locate recent, credible studies and public datasets relevant to the specific market.
  3. Record each source's date, population, methods, and limitations.
  4. Review 3–5 named competitor websites for observable prices, product claims, shipping and returns policies.
  5. Separate direct observations from inferences and unsupported hypotheses.

3. Example competitor comparison template

Brand A / B / C: placeholder names only. A real paid brief would replace these with verified businesses and dated observations.

Fields: product category · listed price · stated sustainability claim · evidence linked by brand · delivery and return terms · observation date.

4. Example decision recommendations

These are suggested next investigations, not conclusions from collected evidence:

  • Test whether customers understand the brand's sustainability claims.
  • Check whether comparable product pricing matches the intended positioning.
  • Identify missing information that would require primary customer research.

5. Limitations and sources

No market data or external sources were analysed for this illustrative layout. A commissioned report would include an explicit source list and evidence table. No market-size, preference or conversion-rate estimates should be inferred from this page.
