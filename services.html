Starter Market Research Brief

For independent brands, small businesses and early-stage founders exploring a specific consumer, competitor or market question.

Illustrative pilot price: US$49–99

Not a confirmed price or an automatic checkout. A final quote and delivery schedule depend on the scope agreed by email.

What a scoped brief may include

  • One agreed research question and target market
  • Desk-based review of publicly available evidence on consumer needs or buying barriers
  • Comparison of 3–5 publicly observable competitors, when relevant sources are available
  • Linked references, evidence limitations and three possible next steps
  • Typically 5–8 pages in English, delivered as a PDF

What is not included

Original consumer surveys, proprietary databases, confidential competitor information, causal claims without suitable evidence, legal or financial advice, or guaranteed commercial outcomes.

How enquiries work

  1. Send your question, country or market, product category and deadline.
  2. We confirm whether the request is feasible using public sources and agree the scope and quote.
  3. Research begins only after both sides agree the terms and an appropriate payment method, if applicable.
